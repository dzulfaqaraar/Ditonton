# Ditonton App Features Report

Ditonton is a comprehensive movie and TV series catalog application developed by Dicoding Indonesia as part of the Flutter Developer Expert course. This report showcases the key features implemented in the application through visual screenshots.

## Movie Features

### 1. Movies Homepage (1.movies_page.png)
**Feature:** Main movies dashboard with categorized content  
**Description:** The homepage displays three main sections - "Now Playing" showing current movies, "Popular" featuring trending titles, and "Top Rated" highlighting critically acclaimed films. The interface features a clean dark theme with movie posters and a navigation bar.

### 2. Movie Detail Page (2.movies_detail.png)
**Feature:** Detailed movie information display  
**Description:** Comprehensive movie details including poster, title, genre, runtime, rating, and plot overview. Features a watchlist button allowing users to save movies for later viewing. The page shows "How to Train Your Dragon" as an example with its fantasy/family/action categorization.

### 3. Popular Movies (3.movies_popular.png)
**Feature:** Popular movies listing  
**Description:** Displays a curated list of currently popular movies with poster images, titles, and brief descriptions. Shows titles like "How to Train Your Dragon," "M3GAN 2.0," "Man with No Past," and "Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba."

### 4. Top Rated Movies (4.movies_top_rated.png)
**Feature:** Top-rated movies collection  
**Description:** Features critically acclaimed and highly-rated films including classics like "The Shawshank Redemption," "The Godfather" series, "Schindler's List," and contemporary hits like "KPop Demon Hunters" and "12 Angry Men."

### 5. Movie Search (5.movies_search_page.png)
**Feature:** Movie search functionality  
**Description:** Allows users to search for specific movies using keywords. The example shows search results for "Harry" displaying Harry Potter films and other related movies with their posters and descriptions.

## TV Series Features

### 6. TV Series Homepage (6.tv_series_page.png)
**Feature:** Main TV series dashboard  
**Description:** Similar to the movies homepage, featuring three categories: "Airing Today" for current shows, "Popular" for trending series, and "Top Rated" for critically acclaimed programs. Shows content like talk shows, anime, and various TV series.

### 7. TV Series Detail Page (7.tv_series_detail.png)
**Feature:** Detailed TV series information  
**Description:** Comprehensive series details including poster, title, genre classification, rating, and show overview. Example shows "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" with comedy/talk genre classification and detailed description.

### 8. Airing Today TV Series (8.tv_series_airing_today.png)
**Feature:** Currently airing TV series  
**Description:** Lists TV shows currently broadcasting, including talk shows like "The Tonight Show," "The Daily Show," cooking shows like "Ici tout commence," and late-night programs like "Late Night with Seth Meyers."

### 9. Popular TV Series (9.tv_series_popular.png)
**Feature:** Popular TV series collection  
**Description:** Showcases trending TV series including anime like "Nukitashi the Animation," talk shows, reality TV, and international content such as Dutch programming "Goede Tijden, Slechte Tijden."

### 10. Top Rated TV Series (10.tv_series_top_rated.png)
**Feature:** Top-rated TV series  
**Description:** Features highly-rated series including "Breaking Bad," anime like "Frieren: Beyond Journey's End" and "Arcane," animated series "Avatar: The Last Airbender," and popular anime "One Piece."

### 11. TV Series Search (11.tv_series_search_page.png)
**Feature:** TV series search functionality  
**Description:** Enables searching for specific TV series. The example shows search results for "One Piece" displaying various related content including different versions and spin-offs of the popular anime series.

### 12. TV Series Episodes (12.tv_series_episode.png)
**Feature:** Episode listing and details  
**Description:** Displays individual episodes of a TV series with episode thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and expand functionality. Shows "One Piece" episodes with detailed episode information and visual previews.

## Universal Features

### 13. Watchlist (13.watchlist_page.png)
**Feature:** Personal watchlist management  
**Description:** Allows users to save and manage their favorite movies and TV series in a personal collection. Shows saved items including "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" and "How to Train Your Dragon" with their respective details.

### 14. About Page (14.about_page.png)
**Feature:** Application information  
**Description:** Provides information about the Ditonton application, explaining that it's a movie catalog app developed by Dicoding Indonesia as a sample project for the Flutter Developer Expert course. Features the company logo and mission statement.

## Technical Highlights

- **Clean UI Design:** Dark theme with intuitive navigation
- **Cross-Platform:** Built with Flutter for mobile platforms
- **Comprehensive Content:** Both movies and TV series support
- **Search Functionality:** Robust search capabilities for both content types
- **Personal Features:** Watchlist functionality for user engagement
- **Detailed Information:** Rich metadata display for all content
- **Categorized Browsing:** Multiple ways to discover content (popular, top-rated, current)
- **Episode Management:** Detailed episode tracking for TV series
